MSc Dissertation · 2026

Environment-Aware Feasibility Planning for Local LLMs in MuJoCo

A MuJoCo tabletop benchmark tests whether local LLMs distinguish executable, infeasible and ambiguous instructions when the language stays fixed but the environment changes.
168tasks
84matched pairs
9,828experiment records
My contribution I designed seven controlled failure categories and implemented a deterministic feasibility verifier. Structured feedback supports up to three rounds of plan repair and blocks plans that remain infeasible from execution.
Evaluation Across six local models and 126 held-out tasks, verified planning reached 95.2–100% matched-pair success for five models; the full configuration recorded 0% simulator crashes. This is planning-feasibility evaluation, not robot policy training.

Team Robotics Project · 2025–2026

V.I.S.O.R. — Autonomous Mobile Manipulation

Our Leo Rover system combines ROS2, SLAM, navigation, RGB-D perception and arm manipulation to search for objects, approach, grasp, return and sort them by colour.
My contribution I primarily tested and tuned the navigation subsystem: SLAM mapping/localisation, Nav2 global and local planners, costmaps, coverage, obstacle handling and return-to-start. I also supported Gazebo and physical Leo Rover integration tests across SLAM, Nav2, TF and the task flow.
